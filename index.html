What is Just One Rupee?

Just One Rupee (JOR) is a community-powered voluntary support initiative created to back the development and growth of Phantomix. Our core philosophy is simple: supporting innovation shouldn't require deep pockets.

Whether someone contributes ₹1, ₹5, ₹10, ₹51, ₹101, or any amount they comfortably choose, every single rupee helps sustain engineering, hosting, and product design. There is zero pressure and zero compulsory amount.

What is Phantomix?

Phantomix is an independent, technology-focused startup project dedicated to building practical web-based tools, digital utilities, and modern applications designed for everyday creators and internet users.

Contributions made to Just One Rupee are unconditional voluntary gifts/support for the Phantomix project. Contributors do not receive shares, equity, profit-sharing, debt repayment, or guaranteed commercial returns.

2. Not a Tax-Exempt Charity

Neither Just One Rupee nor Phantomix is registered as a Section 8 company, trust, or society under Indian law. Contributions are not eligible for Section 80G income tax deductions.

3. Payment Recipient & Bookkeeping

All UPI contributions sent to 8168350829@amazonpay are logged for transparent accounting. Financial summaries showing total received, spent, and remaining balance are published openly on our Home dashboard.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Why didn't the dashboard number increase immediately after I paid?

Because we use direct zero-fee UPI transfers instead of an automated third-party payment gateway, every transaction is manually cross-checked against bank statements before updating the public ledger so that only 100% verified figures appear.

Q2. What if I entered the wrong amount or sent a duplicate payment?

Submit your 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) number in the support form on this page within 7 days of payment, and our team will verify the credit and assist you promptly.

Q3. Can I contribute just ₹1?

Yes! ₹1 is the heart of this campaign. Select ₹1 on the Home page, scan the auto-generated QR code, and tap Done to record your gesture.

Q4. Does Just One Rupee ever message people asking for money?

Never. We never send spam DMs, cold calls, or payment links asking for money. All contributions happen strictly through our official website and social channels.
